How do I add a widget?

Open Affirmosa → Widget Affirmations → pick a widget → "Add to my Home Screen" (or Lock Screen). The app walks you through each step. iOS doesn't let apps place widgets for you.

How often does my widget change?

Each widget has its own setting: at angel numbers (11:11, 2:22…), every hour, three times a day, once a day, or never. Change it in Widget Affirmations → your widget.

How do I set a wallpaper?

Open Wallpapers, choose your words and photo, and tap Save to Photos. Then set it from the Photos app or iOS Settings → Wallpaper. iOS doesn't let apps set your wallpaper directly.

I'm not getting reminders.

Check that iOS Settings → Notifications → Affirmosa is allowed, and that a Focus mode isn't hiding them. Open the app once after changing this so your reminders are rescheduled.

My widget isn't changing.

Open the app once. Widgets refresh from the app, and iOS may delay updates in Low Power Mode.

Free trial and subscription

New subscribers get a free trial, and the app reminds you the day before it ends (if notifications are on). Manage or cancel any time in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends to avoid being charged. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

New phone? Open Affirmosa → Settings → Restore purchases.

If you're having a hard time

Affirmosa is for encouragement, not a substitute for professional help. In the U.S., call or text 988 any time (988lifeline.org). Elsewhere, contact your local emergency number.
