Privacy Policy

Affirmosa · Last updated November 27, 2026

The short version: Affirmosa has no accounts, no ads and no servers of its own. Your name, your answers, your favorites and the affirmations you write stay on your phone. We count anonymous app events (like "a widget was set up") to improve the App, without your IP address or location. We never sell or share your information.

This policy explains what Affirmosa ("the App") does with information. The App is made by Morning Harbor LLC, a Texas limited liability company ("we", "us").

What stays on your phone

These are stored only on your device, in the App's private storage. They are never sent to us, and we cannot see them:

  • The name you type, if you give one, and your answers to the welcome questions (what you're working through and toward, your preferred tone and wording).
  • The categories you choose, your favorites and collections, and any affirmations you write or edit.
  • Your widget, wallpaper, reminder, sound and app icon settings.

Widgets read the same private storage on your phone. Reminders, including angel-number reminders and the note before a free trial ends, are scheduled by your phone. Nothing is sent from a server.

Photos

When you save a wallpaper or a picture, the App asks permission to add it to your Photos. It does not read, scan or upload your photo library.

iCloud

A small record of the days you used the App may be saved in your own iCloud account through Apple, so it carries over to a new phone. Apple stores it under your Apple Account. We cannot see it.

Age range

Where the law requires it, the App asks Apple for your age range (not your birthday). Apple handles this on your device. It is not sent to us.

Anonymous app statistics

We use PostHog, an analytics service, to count anonymous events so we can see which parts of the App help people and which don't. They are:

  • Setup: which welcome screens were shown, the topics picked, how often widgets should change, and where you heard about the App if you choose to answer (for example "TikTok" or "A friend").
  • Features used: which tabs are opened, widget and wallpaper settings (for example "Lock Screen, daily"), reminders turned on (how many, not their times' wording), the feed category chosen, and that an affirmation was saved, shared or downloaded (not which one).
  • Subscriptions: whether the subscription screen was shown and led to a purchase, and which plan.
  • App opens, installs and updates.

Each event also carries basic phone details: the App version, phone model, iOS version, language and region setting, and time zone. Each install gets a random ID that is not linked to your name, email, device identifiers or advertising ID.

We do not store your internet (IP) address or your location. We never send your name, what you write, or the words of the affirmations you see. PostHog stores this data in the United States. See PostHog's privacy policy.

Subscriptions

Payments are handled by Apple. We never see your name, email or card details.

To check whether your subscription is active, the App uses RevenueCat, a subscription service. RevenueCat receives a random ID created for your copy of the App (not your name or email), your purchase and renewal history, and basic technical details sent with every request: your IP address, device model, iOS and App version, language and App Store country. RevenueCat stores this in the United States and uses it only to provide its service to us. It does not receive anything you type in the App. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, sign-in or email collection.
  • No advertising, ad tracking or advertising identifier, and no data brokers.
  • No analytics that read what you write, and no IP address or location stored.
  • We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not use it to profile you.

Why we use this information, and for how long

We use purchase information only to provide the subscription you asked for (for people in the UK and EU, the legal basis is performance of a contract). We use anonymous app statistics to understand and improve the App (legitimate interests); PostHog keeps them for up to seven years. RevenueCat keeps purchase records while the App uses its service and as long as needed for accounting and legal duties. Everything on your phone stays until you delete it or delete the App. Settings → Erase my data clears it from the App.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what information is held about you, to have it corrected or deleted, or to object to how it is used. Because almost everything stays on your phone, deleting the App removes it. For anything held by RevenueCat, email us and we will help. If you are in the UK or EU, you may also complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO).

International transfers

We are based in the United States, and RevenueCat and PostHog store their data there. If you use the App outside the U.S., that information is transferred to the U.S., where RevenueCat protects it under its data processing terms.

Not medical advice

Affirmations are for encouragement. They are not medical, mental health, financial or legal advice. If you are struggling, please reach out to someone you trust or a professional. In the U.S. or Canada, call or text 988 any time. Elsewhere, find a free helpline at findahelpline.com. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call your local emergency number (911 in the U.S. and Canada, 999 in the UK, 000 in Australia, 111 in New Zealand).

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If the App's data practices change, we will update this page and the date above, and say so in the App if the change is significant.
